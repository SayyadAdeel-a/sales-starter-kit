# The 30-Day Sales Starter Action Plan & Daily Operating Cadence

Thinking about sales will not make you a salesperson. Starting real conversations will. 

This guide outlines the daily operating system and a structured 4-week roadmap to go from zero sales experience to active pipeline momentum.

---

## The Daily Sales Time-Blocking Architecture

Salesforce research reveals that salespeople spend only **28% of their working hours actually selling**. The rest disappears into disorganized email checking, scattered admin, internal chatter, and chasing missing information. 

High performers protect their calendar with disciplined blocks:

## The 30-Day Action Roadmap

```
Week 1: LEARN ──► Week 2: PRACTICE ──► Week 3: REACH OUT ──► Week 4: FOLLOW UP
```

### Week 1: Learn & Target
- **Objective**: Establish domain clarity and build a foundation before messaging anyone.
- **Milestones**:
  1. Complete the Path Diagnostic (Choose B2B, B2C, or Independent).
  2. Pick **10 specific target companies** or businesses in that path you admire.
  3. Study their business models: *How do they generate revenue? Who is their ideal customer? What primary problem do they solve?*
  4. Optimize your LinkedIn or primary social profile:
     - Clear, professional headshot.
     - A 1-sentence value proposition headline (e.g., *"Helping [Target Buyer] solve [Problem] with [Solution]"*).
     - Remove clutter and unprofessional posts.

### Week 2: Practice & Conversation
- **Objective**: Overcome hesitation by starting low-stakes, real conversations.
- **Milestones**:
  1. Select something small to sell: a service you can perform (research, copywriting, design, scheduling), an affiliate product, or offer to help a friend sell their service.
  2. Initiate **20 genuine conversations** on LinkedIn, Twitter, or WhatsApp.
  3. Enforce the **two ears, one mouth rule**: Ask diagnostic questions about what they find difficult; do not pitch or pressure them.
  4. Count and log every response. Experience getting ignored and realize it isn't personal.

### Week 3: Reach Out & Network
- **Objective**: Build professional relationships and apply for entry opportunities.
- **Milestones**:
  1. Identify 5 professionals currently working in your target role (e.g., SDRs or account managers in software companies).
  2. Send a short, humble note asking for advice:
     > *"Hi [Name], really admire your career trajectory at [Company]. I'm preparing to enter the sales field and would love to ask: what is one thing you wish you knew when you were starting out? No worries if your schedule is packed!"*
  3. Submit applications to **10 entry-level roles** (SDR/BDR, sales consultant, or remote setter).
  4. Accompany every single application with a customized 3-sentence note referencing their product and target market.

### Week 4: Follow Up & Audit Ratios
- **Objective**: Close open loops and establish your permanent tracking cadence.
- **Milestones**:
  1. Follow up on every application and outreach message sent in Week 3 across a second channel if appropriate.
  2. Maintain polite persistence—remember that people are busy, not rejecting you.
  3. Reconcile your 30-day conversion funnel:
     - Total Conversations Started: `_____`
     - Total Replies Received: `_____`
     - Discovery Calls / Meetings Held: `_____`
     - Proposals Sent / Interviews Booked: `_____`
  4. Identify your single biggest bottleneck (Were you getting low replies? Were conversations stalling before calls?) and set your 30-day improvement goal.

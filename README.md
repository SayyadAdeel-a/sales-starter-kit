## 💡 Overview

Most sales training and AI sales prompts fail because they rely on manipulative closing tricks, high-pressure scripts, and outdated tropes. 

This skill transforms any AI assistant into an elite consultative sales coach and operator, based on **The Sales Starter Kit** by **Mahdi Shafiei** (Founder of Trifid Media / Money Machine).

### Core Philosophy:
1. **Helping, Not Tricking**: Sales is helping someone make a decision that is good for them and good for the business you represent.
2. **The Numbers Funnel**: Sales is an empirical numbers game (`100 conversations → 5 meetings → 3 proposals → 1 client`). When you know your ratios, rejection stops feeling personal.
3. **Autonomy Beats Pressure**: High-pressure tactics trigger psychological reactance. Giving buyers explicit autonomy (*"You are free to say no"*) significantly increases compliance and trust.

## 🎯 Core Frameworks & Capabilities

### 1. The 8-Stage "Lead to Money" Pipeline
Every transaction—from a B2C appointment to an enterprise B2B deal—progresses across 8 sequential stages:

```
1. Lead ──► 2. Contact ──► 3. Conversation ──► 4. Qualification
                                                      │
8. Follow-up ◄── 7. Payment ◄── 6. Decision ◄── 5. Offer
```

- **Stages 1–4**: Where 80% of revenue is won or lost (identifying fit, rapid human contact, two-way discovery).
- **Stages 5–6**: The offer and decision pivot.
- **Stages 7–8**: Seamless checkout and the compound engine of repeat deals and referrals.

### 2. Speed-to-Lead Advantage
- **7× Advantage**: According to Harvard Business Review research, responding to an inquiry within **1 hour** makes you **7 times more likely** to qualify the lead than waiting just 60 minutes longer.
- Effort and discipline beat tenure on day one.

### 3. The 3 Career Pathways
Detailed career ladders, pay models, and daily cadences for:
- **Path 1: B2B (Businesses)** — Selling to companies (`SDR/BDR → AE → Account Manager → Head of Sales`). High stability, base salary + commission.
- **Path 2: B2C (People)** — High-ticket personal purchases (Real estate, automotive, fitness, education). High commission upside, fast cycles.
- **Path 3: Independent & Social** — Remote appointment setting, closing, or selling personal offers. Uncapped upside and geographic freedom.

### 4. The 8 Core Competencies & Zero-Experience Playbook
Proven frameworks to demonstrate all 8 key sales traits during interviews without previous sales experience:
1. *Listening & Curiosity* (Asking follow-ups; Harvard study)
2. *Resilience* (Goal persistence stories)
3. *Clear Communication* (Buyer-centric writing)
4. *Organization & Follow-Through* (Same-day follow-up)
5. *Coachability* (Feedback absorption)
6. *Business Understanding* (One-sentence revenue models)
7. *Comfort with Numbers* (Activity metric tracking)
8. *Digital & Social Presence* (Profile positioning)

### 5. Science-Backed Autonomy Objection Handling
- **Ambivert Advantage**: Research by Dr. Adam Grant demonstrates that ambiverts generate **32% more revenue** than extroverts.
- **"But You Are Free" (BYAF) Technique**: Meta-analysis across 42 studies (Carpenter, 2013) reveals that affirming prospect autonomy doubles agreement.
- **Challenger Selling**: Top performers teach prospects new insights about their business instead of relying solely on relationship-building.

### 6. Protecting the 28% & 30-Day Action Roadmap
- **The 28% Problem**: Salesforce data indicates reps spend only 28% of their time actively selling. This skill provides a strict 5-part daily time-blocking schedule.
- **4-Week Action Plan**:
  - *Week 1 (Learn)*: Path selection, target account list, and profile optimization.
  - *Week 2 (Practice)*: 20 low-stakes diagnostic conversations.
  - *Week 3 (Reach Out)*: Informational interviews and 10 tailored applications.
  - *Week 4 (Follow Up & Audit)*: Multi-channel follow-ups and conversion funnel audit.

## 🛠 Installation & Setup

### Option 1: Google Antigravity / Gemini CLI (Global Installation)

Clone this repository directly into your Antigravity global skills directory:

```bash
# Windows (PowerShell)
git clone https://github.com/<your-username>/sales-starter-kit.git "$env:USERPROFILE\.gemini\config\skills\sales-starter-kit"

# macOS / Linux
git clone https://github.com/<your-username>/sales-starter-kit.git ~/.gemini/config/skills/sales-starter-kit
```

### Option 2: Claude Code

Add this repository to your Claude Code skills or configuration directory:

```bash
git clone https://github.com/<your-username>/sales-starter-kit.git ~/.claude/skills/sales-starter-kit
```

### Option 3: Project-Local Integration

Clone or copy the folder directly into your workspace's `.gemini/skills/` or `skills/` directory.

## 💬 Example Prompts

Once installed, your agent will automatically activate the skill whenever you ask questions like:

- **Career Guidance**:
  > *"I want to start a career in sales, but I don't know whether B2B or B2C fits me better. Walk me through the diagnostic self-check."*
- **Interview Preparation**:
  > *"I have an interview for a junior BDR role next Tuesday, but I have zero sales background. How do I prove coachability and business understanding?"*
- **Cold Outreach**:
  > *"Help me draft a short, 3-sentence cold LinkedIn connection message to a Founder using autonomy framing and no cheesy sales lines."*
- **Objection Handling**:
  > *"A prospect replied: 'Your price is too high compared to our current vendor.' Draft an empathetic response using the L.A.C.E. method."*
- **Pipeline Strategy**:
  > *"I need to close 2 clients this month. Based on the 100 → 5 → 3 → 1 ratio, what should my daily outreach targets be?"*
